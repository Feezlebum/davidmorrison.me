01 The brief

This is bigger than running a community channel.

The job is to give World Foundation and World Chain a credible voice, carry complex work into the conversations that matter, and build enough trust that builders, researchers, founders, investors, and media want to stay in the room.

01 Set the narrative Turn protocol, governance, grants, privacy, and ecosystem work into a clear point of view that is recognisably the Foundation's.
02 Earn relevance Know which conversations, communities, events, and relationships matter, then contribute with substance instead of chasing attention.
03 Connect the ecosystem Keep Foundation, core contributors, builders, partners, and community aligned without flattening their different roles.

03 Why the fit is real

Foundation perspective

I spent nearly four years at NEAR Foundation, progressing through Community Lead, Community Engagement Lead, Head of Social Media, and Head of Creative Campaigns & Social Media. I learned how to represent a network rather than a single product, and how to work across leadership, ecosystem teams, partners, developers, and community.

Technical translation

At Douro Labs I lead social for special projects across Layer 1 infrastructure and onchain perpetuals. The work starts with founders, engineers, and product teams, then turns market structure, matching systems, Layer 1 architecture, and consensus design into language that stays accurate.

Community infrastructure

I set the strategy for NEAR's forum and Discord, which both reached all-time activity highs during my tenure. Earlier, I ran Discord, Telegram, BitcoinTalk, and Reddit at Plutus and built its ambassador programme and customer-support system from scratch.

People and presence

I hired and mentored a Marketing Executive through to independent ownership, worked with partners and agencies across time zones, and represented NEAR at conferences and ecosystem events around the world, both on stage and in the room.

AI from the inside

I build products with AI rather than talking about it from a distance. My live projects include an AI mascot tool, an autonomous comic, and consumer apps built through AI-assisted product development. At NEAR I also worked directly on brand strategy with Illia Polosukhin, co-author of “Attention Is All You Need.”

05 Why World

The internet needs a way to know when a human is present without asking that human to give up who they are.

That is a difficult technical story, a live cultural argument, and a coordination problem across builders, institutions, and communities. It is exactly the kind of work I enjoy: learn the details, find the honest version of the story, and make sure the organisation shows up where the argument is actually happening.
